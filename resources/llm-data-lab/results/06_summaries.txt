=== B: Can Bitcoin mining increase renewable electricity capacity?
  abstract (383 chars): Supporters of Bitcoin claim that miners' demand for electricity leads to more renewable capacity. We estimate a Bitcoin electricity demand curve and add it to a long-run model of the Texas electricity market. Bitcoin mining does increase renewable capacity, but it also increases carbon emissions. When miners offer demand response to the grid, most of the extra emissions disappear.
